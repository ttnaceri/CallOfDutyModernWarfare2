MODERN WARFARE 3D - Tom's campaign (three.js, works offline)
Run: open index.html in Chrome/Edge/Firefox. The main menu lets you pick any of the 17 missions.
Squad: Ghost, James, Price. You are Tom.
Missions: 1 Breach, 2 Night Raid, 3 Overwatch, 4 Final Stand (+favela escape), 5 Cliffhanger (+snowmobile ramp jump),
6 Aircraft Carrier, 7 Door Gunner, 8 Gunship Strike, 9 Drone Strike, 10 Runway Race, 11 Storm Front, 12 Ship Breaker,
13 HALO Jump, 14 The Only Easy Day... Was Yesterday (opening cinematic, Enter/Space skips), 15 Oilrig, 16 Black Raven,
17 General Shepherd (boat chase, helicopter chase, waterfall, duel; add new missions before it).
Controls: C = first-person view in vehicles/drone (crouch on foot), Alt = weapon workshop, K = binoculars, F = interact/board/jump.
