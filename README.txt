MODERN WARFARE 3D - Rouch's campaign (three.js, works offline)
Run: open index.html in Chrome/Edge/Firefox. The main menu lets you pick any of the 17 missions.
Missions: 1 Breach, 2 Night Raid, 3 Overwatch, 4 Final Stand (+favela rooftop escape), 5 Cliffhanger (+snowmobile ramp jump to the far shore),
6 Aircraft Carrier (jet bombing), 7 Door Gunner, 8 Gunship Strike, 9 Drone Strike (UAV cam, Hellfire missiles),
10 Runway Race (catch the flying plane, F to board), 11 Storm Front, 12 Ship Breaker, 13 HALO Jump (F to jump, right-click parachute, then ground fight),
14 The Only Easy Day... Was Yesterday, 15 Oilrig, 16 Black Raven, 17 General Shepherd (final, melee duel; add new missions before it).
Vehicles: press C while driving/flying for first-person view. On foot C = crouch, Left-Ctrl = prone.
Weapons: 1 rifle, 2 pistol, 3 knife, 4 sniper, 5 RPG (homing), 6 shotgun, 7 SMG. Alt = weapon workshop (incl. Thermal). K = binoculars.
